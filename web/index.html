Features

90 original cards

30 per hero: attacks, skills and powers, each with an upgraded version. Win runs to unlock more.

Read the enemy

Every enemy shows its intent: attack, defend, empower or curse. Plan Block, Energy and statuses around it.

Branching maps

Fights, elites, campfires, merchants, mystery events and treasure on a new map every act.

23 relics, 3 bosses

The Rootmother, the Glass Tyrant and the Unwoven guard the top of each act.

How to play

  • Drag a card onto an enemy to play it, or tap a card and then tap a target.
  • Each turn you get 3 Energy and draw 5 cards. Block lasts until your next turn.
  • Keyboard: 1-9 select, Tab target, Enter play, E end turn, Esc pause.
  • Your run saves every time you reach the map. Your run history and best score are kept too.

Built with

Godot 4.3 (GDScript, Compatibility renderer), exported to WebAssembly. Every picture is drawn in code and every sound is synthesized at startup - no image or audio files. Source on GitHub.
